# Code of conduct

maqati3 deals with the words of scholars, and the people working on it come from
different backgrounds and levels of experience. We hold ourselves to the manners that
material deserves.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient with everyone, especially with people new to programming, to Git,
  or to this project.
- Assume good intent. Criticise code and ideas, never the person who wrote them.
- Keep discussions on the work: code, data, documentation and process.
- When you are corrected, take it as help. When you correct someone, make it easy to
  accept.

## What this repository is not for

- **Debating rulings, scholars, groups or methodology.** The project's job is to show
  accurately what was said and where. It is not to decide who is right. Issues, pull
  requests and comments that turn into such debates will be closed.
- **Speaking about any person with contempt**, whether a scholar, a contributor or
  anyone else. That includes insults and accusations against people.
- **Harassment of any kind**: personal attacks, intimidation, sexual language or
  advances, publishing someone's private information, or spam.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit, hide or delete comments, close issues and pull requests, and
block people from the repository who do not respect this code.

To report a problem, mention the maintainer,
[@PSchmitz-Valckenberg](https://github.com/PSchmitz-Valckenberg), or, for something that
should not be discussed in public, use GitHub's *Report content* on the comment
concerned. Reports are handled discreetly.
